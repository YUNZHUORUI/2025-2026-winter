\section{Verification Methods Summary}

Requirements will be verified using a combination of complementary methods to ensure both functional and performance compliance:

\begin{description}[noitemsep]
    \item[Test (T):] Direct flight tests, thrust stand measurements, endurance trials, and subsystem bench tests under representative operating conditions.
    \item[Analysis (A):] Mathematical modelling and simulation (ANSYS Mechanical, Fluent CFD) as well as hand calculations to validate aerodynamic, structural, and energy performance.
    \item[Inspection (I):] Visual and dimensional checks against CAD drawings, datasheets, and tolerance specifications to confirm manufacturing accuracy.
    \item[Demonstration (D):] Full-system demonstrations during design reviews to verify VTOL capability, tilt-lock operation, autonomous navigation, and telemetry functions.
\end{description}

Kinematic and structural simulation (ANSYS Mechanical / Fluent) will serve as the \textbf{primary analysis tool} for verifying FR-002 (VTOL-to-cruise transition), PR-011 (maximum range), and all structural requirements defined in Phase~3.
