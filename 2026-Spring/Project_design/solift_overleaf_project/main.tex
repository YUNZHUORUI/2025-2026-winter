\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This report presents a comprehensive Design for Manufacturing (DFM) analysis of the
\textbf{Solift} -- a Three-Axis Solar-Powered Tilt-Rotor Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) --
developed as part of the Technion DFM course project. The Solift platform is conceived as
a heavy-lift heterogeneous VTOL (H-VTOL) logistics drone capable of vertical take-off and
landing, seamless transition to efficient fixed-wing cruise flight, and extended-range
operations augmented by integrated photovoltaic energy harvesting.

The report covers eight primary sections: object selection and justification through a
comparative matrix analysis; a formal Systems Requirements Review (SRR) document; a
detailed concept design encompassing materials selection and solid modelling; a comprehensive 
technical specification data sheet; a dedicated structural and aerodynamic simulation (FEA/CFD) 
analysis; and a Preliminary Design Review (PDR). The design combines a
prepreg carbon fibre / Nomex honeycomb airframe, aerospace-grade 7075-T6 aluminium tilt
mechanisms, and flexible IBC solar cells encapsulated in ETFE film to achieve a maximum
take-off weight of \SI{12.0}{kg} with a \SI{5.0}{kg} payload capacity and a target range
exceeding \SI{120}{km} under illuminated conditions.
